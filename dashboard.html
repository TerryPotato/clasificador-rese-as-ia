🔧 Fine-tuning Techniques Applied

🛑 Early Stopping

Stopped training at epoch 5 when Validation Loss stopped improving for 2 consecutive epochs. Prevents overfitting.

📉 Cosine LR Scheduler

Learning rate gradually decreased following a cosine curve, allowing finer learning in later stages.

✂️ Gradient Clipping

Limits gradients to max_norm=1.0 to prevent them from exploding and destabilizing the model.

⚡ BF16 Precision

Brain Float 16 training to maximize RTX 5060 Ti performance. More stable than FP16 for FLAN-T5.
